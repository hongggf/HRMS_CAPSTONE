# 📘 HRMS User Manual

Welcome to your new Human Resources Management System (HRMS)! This guide will walk you through how to use the system step-by-step, whether you are setting up the company structure, recruiting candidates, or making final executive approvals.

---

## 🏢 Part 1: Setting up the Company (For HR Admins)
Before you can hire anyone, you need to define your company's structure.

### How to Create a Department
1. Log into your dashboard and navigate to the **Organization** tab.
2. Click **Add Department**.
3. Enter the department name (e.g., "Engineering") and a short code (e.g., "ENG").
4. Click **Save**. 

### How to Create a Position
Once your departments exist, you can define the jobs within them.
1. Navigate to the **Positions** page.
2. Click **Create Position**.
3. Select the Department this role belongs to (e.g., Engineering).
4. Enter the Job Title (e.g., "Software Engineer") and the approved salary range.
5. Click **Save**.

---

## 🙋 Part 2: Asking for New Hires (For HR Admins)
When a manager tells you they need to hire someone, you must submit a formal request.

### How to Request Headcount
1. Go to the **Workforce Planning** tab.
2. Click **New Workforce Request**.
3. Select the Department and Position you want to hire for.
4. Enter the number of people you need (Headcount) and a justification (e.g., "Needed for the upcoming Q3 project").
5. Click **Submit Request**. 
*Your request will now be sent to the Head of HR for budget approval.*

---

## 👑 Part 3: Approving Budgets & Headcount (For Head of HR)
As the Head of HR, you control the budget. No one can recruit for a role until you sign off on it.

### How to Approve a Workforce Request
1. Log into your dashboard and check your **Approvals Inbox**.
2. Click on a **Pending Workforce Request**.
3. Review the requested position, headcount, and justification.
4. If it looks good, click **Approve**. 
*(If the budget is tight, you can click **Reject** and provide a reason).*

---

## 🤝 Part 4: Recruiting Candidates (For HR Recruiters)
Once the Head of HR approves a request, the Recruitment team takes over to find the perfect candidate.

### How to Post a Job to the Public
1. Go to the **Recruitment** tab.
2. Look for an "Approved Workforce Request" and click **Create Job Requisition**.
3. Click **Create Job Posting**.
4. Write out the job description, requirements, and location.
5. Change the status to **Published**. The job is now live on your company careers page!

### How to Manage Applicants
As candidates apply on your careers page, their resumes will appear in your system.
1. Go to the **Applications** dashboard.
2. Click on a candidate's profile to read their resume.
3. Update their status as you move them through the process:
   * Select **Screening** when you are reviewing them.
   * Select **Shortlisted** if they look like a great fit.

### How to Interview & Select a Candidate
1. For a shortlisted candidate, click **Schedule Interview**. 
2. Choose the date, time, and the person who will be interviewing them.
3. After the interview finishes, the interviewer logs in and fills out the **Evaluation Form** (scoring their strengths and weaknesses).
4. Once you have found the perfect person, change their application status to **Selected**.
5. Finally, change the status to **Pending Head of HR Approval** to send their file up the chain for the final signature.

---

## 🖋️ Part 5: Finalizing the Hire (For Head of HR)
Before an offer letter can be sent, the Head of HR must review the recruiter's choice.

### How to Give Final Approval
1. Open your **Approvals Inbox** again.
2. Click on a **Pending Hire**.
3. Review the candidate's resume, their interview scores, and the recruiter's notes.
4. Click **Approve Candidate**.
5. Congratulations! The candidate is now officially approved and ready to begin their Onboarding.
